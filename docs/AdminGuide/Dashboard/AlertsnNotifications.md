# Alerts and Notifications
The **Alerts and Notifications** section on Dashboard keeps you informed about important updates and system activities in real time. This feature ensures that critical information, such as device alerts, maintenance notifications, or system status changes, is promptly delivered to users, helping them stay proactive and in control. With clear categorization and intuitive design, this section enables efficient monitoring and swift action, supporting seamless management of electric vehicle charging operations.
Navigate to **Dashboard** > **Alerts & Notifications**. The following screen appears that shows the Alerts and Notifications under their respective tabs.
