# About Revenue Share

The HIEV platform is designed to benefit all stakeholders in the EV charging ecosystem. By enabling revenue sharing, the platform fosters collaboration between charging station owners, operators, partners, or any other stakeholders (also known as entities) involved in running the business. In a partnership-driven approach, the station owners can share a portion of the revenue generated from charging sessions with these entities. 

The HIEV platform's robust dashboard and reporting tools enable real-time and automated tracking of revenue and its distribution. This transparency builds trust among the entities and ensures seamless operations.
