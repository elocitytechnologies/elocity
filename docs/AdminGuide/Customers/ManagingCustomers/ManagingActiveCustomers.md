## Viewing Active Customers

To view all the active customers in the system, follow these steps:, navigate to **Customer** > **Customers**. The following screen appears that displays all the active customers under the **Active Customers** tab.![Customers](img/Customers1.png)
## Viewing Customer Details
Click anywhere on the customer's record that you want to view. The following screen appears that shows all the customer details:![View Customer](img/ViewCustomer.png)
## Editing Customer Details
1. Click on the **Edit** button. The following screen appears:![Edit Customer](img/Edit.png)
2. Under the Info tab, make the desired changes.
3. Click **Save**.

## Managing RFID Details
1. Navigate to the **RFID** tab, the following screen appears:![RFID](img/RFID1.png)
	- To associate a new RFID with a customer, click the **Add RFID** button, provide all the necessary details and click **Save**.![Add RFID](img/AddRFID.png)
	- To edit the RFID details associated with a customer, select **Edit** from the **Action** drop-down list. In the screen that appears, make the desired changes, then click Save.![Edit RFID](img/RFID2.png)
	- To delete the RFID associated with the customer, select **Delete** from the **Action** drop-down list.
